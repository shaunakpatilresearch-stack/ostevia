      About

      What ReFix is

      In many hospitals in Africa and South Asia, plates are removed from healed patients, cleaned, and used again because patients cannot afford new ones. Surgeons confirm the practice is widespread, the only laboratory data says used plates fail sooner, and no guideline or registry exists. ReFix gives the surgeon a transparent go/no-go at the moment of decision and records every reuse so that outcome data finally accumulates.

      What it is not

      It does not certify a plate as safe. Nobody can know how many load cycles a used plate has absorbed, so ReFix classifies risk, keeps high-load uses off the table, and shows its reasoning for the surgeon to overrule. Automatic crack detection is not in this prototype. The bow index from a photo is experimental.

      How to try the prototype

      1. Open this page on a phone. On iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android use the browser menu, then Install or Add to Home screen.
      2. Choose Assess a plate. Pick a plate and a site, answer the checklist. Try an infected-explant answer and watch the verdict reject, with its source.
      3. Photograph any plate-shaped object on white paper to see the bow index. A bent ruler works.
      4. Save the record, open Registry, open it, and add a six-week follow-up.
      5. Copy CSV pastes the whole registry into a spreadsheet.
      6. Records live on that phone only. Reset demo data restores the synthetic set.
