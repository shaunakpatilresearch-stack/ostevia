Sources
    Purpose
    Across Africa and South Asia, plates are taken from healed patients and used again because new ones are unaffordable. Lab tests suggest used plates can be weaker, but there is no validated guideline and no record of how reused plates perform. Ostevia gives a transparent go/no-go, shows the rule and source behind it, and records every reuse and its outcome.
    Limits
    Not a certification. Load history is unknowable, so Ostevia classifies risk, excludes high-load sites, and lets the surgeon overrule. Crack detection is not implemented. The bow index is experimental and is recorded only; it never changes the determination. Plate dimensions are nominal placeholders until verified against manufacturer catalogues.
    Try it
    1. Add to Home Screen on iPhone or Android.
    2. Start an assessment. Change Origin to an infected site and watch the determination reject.
    3. Photograph a ruler on white paper to record a bow index.
    4. Record consent, accept, open the record in Registry, add a follow-up. Records stay on this device.
    5. Search the Registry by ID, plate, site, hospital, finding or outcome, for example "nonunion" or "pitting".
