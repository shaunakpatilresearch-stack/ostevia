Why

    Site fit

    This is decision support. The verdict shows its rules and sources so the surgeon can disagree with it. It does not certify an implant as safe.

    Registry

    Rules engine, draft 0.1

    Every verdict is produced by these rules and nothing else. They are a draft for review by surgeons who reuse implants, and they are deliberately conservative.

    Site load classes

    A reused plate is never cleared for class 3. Class 2 needs a plate with no downgrades. Class 1 is the floor for any plate that passes.

    Plate catalogue

    A plate starts at the highest load class it is catalogued for, capped at class 2 because reused plates never go to class 3. Section modulus at a screw hole is shown for comparison; finite-element fatigue classes per design are the planned upgrade.

    Sources

      What ReFix is

      In many hospitals in Africa and South Asia, plates are removed from healed patients, cleaned, and used again because patients cannot afford new ones. Surgeons confirm the practice is widespread, the only lab data says used plates fail sooner, and no guideline or registry exists. ReFix gives the surgeon a transparent go/no-go at the moment of decision and records every reuse so that outcome data finally accumulates.

      What it is not

      It does not certify a plate as safe. Nobody can know how many load cycles a used plate has absorbed, so ReFix classifies risk, keeps high-load uses off the table, and shows its reasoning for the surgeon to overrule. Automatic crack detection is not in this prototype; the bow index from a photo is experimental.

      How to test this prototype

      1. Open the link on a phone. On iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android use the browser menu, then Add to Home screen or Install.
      2. Tap New plate assessment. Pick a plate and a site, then answer the checklist. Try an infected-explant answer and watch the verdict reject with its source.
      3. Photograph any plate-shaped object on white paper to see the bow index. A bent ruler works.
      4. Save the record, open Registry, tap it, and add a six-week follow-up.
      5. Copy CSV pastes the whole registry into a spreadsheet.
      6. Records live on that phone only. Reset demo data restores the synthetic set.
